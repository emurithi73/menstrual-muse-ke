Who we are

A public-facing space for menstrual health education.

Menstrual Muse is built around education, advocacy, dignity and community engagement. The approach is practical: explain the body, challenge harmful myths, make support visible and connect people to appropriate care.

Our mission

To improve menstrual health and hygiene through education, advocacy, access to menstrual products, and community engagement.

Our vision

A society where menstruation is understood, accepted, and never becomes a barrier to education, health, confidence, or success.

“A period should never cost a girl her future.”
