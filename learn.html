The menstrual cycle

The handbook describes an average adult cycle as 21–35 days, with menstrual, follicular, ovulation and luteal phases.

⌁

Hormones

FSH supports follicle growth; LH triggers ovulation; estrogen builds the uterine lining; progesterone helps maintain it. Falling estrogen and progesterone are associated with menstruation beginning.

♡

Menstrual hygiene

Change pads every 3–4 hours or sooner when soaked, wash hands before and after changing products, bathe regularly and dispose of used pads safely.

+

When to seek care

The handbook highlights concerns including painful or heavy periods, irregular periods, PMS, PMDD, PCOS, endometriosis and fibroids. Persistent symptoms, fever, severe pain, itching or foul-smelling discharge warrant medical attention.

FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

These answers are taken from the supplied handbook rather than invented for the website.

Can I exercise during my period?

Yes. The handbook says exercise is possible during menstruation.

Can I swim while menstruating?

Yes, with suitable menstrual products.

Can pregnancy happen during a period?

It is possible.

Is bathing safe during menstruation?

Yes.
